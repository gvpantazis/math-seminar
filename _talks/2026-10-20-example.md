## Abstract

We introduce variational problems with nonstandard growth, focusing on the double-phase energy

\[
H(x,t)=t^p+a(x)t^q.
\]

We discuss the associated function spaces and explain why the regularity of the coefficient \(a(x)\) plays an important role.
